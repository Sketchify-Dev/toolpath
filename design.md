# Design

## Philosophy (from the user)

- Get inspiration from Mobbin, Awwwards, or 21st.dev.
- Iterate over an existing design rather than inventing from scratch.
- Functional design over fancy design. I am not a designer.
- No em dashes, no en dashes. Use commas, periods, separate sentences.
- No AI slop vocabulary (crucial, delve, enhance, foster, showcase, tapestry, etc.).
- No boldface overuse on proper nouns. Sentence-case headings.
- Plain speech: say what it does, not how it feels.

## Door policy

Walk through two-way doors and show me after. Stop at one-way doors.

Two-way door: anything you can undo. Code, branches, PRs, test data, dev infra.
One-way door: anything you can't. Prod data, deploys to users, force-pushing a shared branch, deleting what you didn't make, a message sent to another person.

If my answer would be a rubber stamp, don't ask. If running something could answer the question, run it instead. Save questions for taste and product calls only I can make. Ask one at a time, with your recommendation.
